# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Startup founders and small teams (DACH, primarily Munich/remote) who want to buy outcomes, not manage developers: scrapers and data pipelines, AI automation, and beautiful websites that ship in weeks. They read fast, value proof over promises, and are addressed with "du".

## Product Purpose

Personal consulting landing page for Amerigo Velletti (Velletti Consulting, ame.velletti.de). It exists to convert founder traffic into contact-form messages and €99 Erstberatung checkouts. Success = inquiries from people who already understand what they're buying.

## Brand Personality

Innovative startup builder. Three words: fast, hands-on, confident. The voice of someone who ships scrapers and beautiful webpages himself — founder tempo, no agency process, no corporate consulting speak.

## Anti-references

- Enterprise consulting sites (Accenture-style "Lösungen für Ihre digitale Transformation").
- Generic freelancer portfolios with skill-percentage bars and stock handshake photos.
- AI-slop landing pages: gradient text, glass cards, hero metrics, identical icon-card grids.

## Design Principles

1. Practice what you preach — the site itself is the proof of "beautiful webpages".
2. Proof over promises — real wins (Enactus World Cup, YC studio, family IT business), no invented logos or testimonials.
3. One person, full responsibility — copy always speaks as "ich" to "du", never "wir/Sie".
4. Founder tempo — short sentences, concrete outcomes ("Wochen, nicht Quartale"), one CTA per fold.
5. Zero-dependency craft — pure PHP/CSS, dark "Midnight Graphite + Gold" system already committed; identity-preservation wins over redesign.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. German-language audience (de primary). Keyboard navigable, reduced-motion honored, semantic HTML, no-JS content visibility. Contact form must remain usable without JavaScript.
